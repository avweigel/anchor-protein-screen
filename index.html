The five proposed criteria, and what the data can say

Anchor proteins are a small set, 5 to 10, tagged in U2-OS so the same proteins can be found across datasets: ours and the ones that already exist. The five headings are the proposed criteria from the Space working group pre-read. Under each, the columns are what public databases can answer today, scored 0, 1 or 2. A low score is something to weigh, not a reason to drop a protein.

Identity

A clear structure in EM, and sources agree on where it sits in U2-OS.

Structure in EM. HPA's main location and UniProt's text are read separately and the higher read counts. Why higher and not an average: HPA has no image for about 7,000 proteins, and a missing image is not evidence that a protein is diffuse; an average would punish exactly those. Whether the two sources agree is scored separately, below. The full term-by-term mapping is at the bottom of this window. 2: a clear structure (nuclear envelope, mitochondrial membrane, Golgi, centriole, lysosome…). 1: a broad or mixed class (vesicles, cytoskeleton, chromosome, cell projection). 0: diffuse (cytosol, nucleoplasm) or no location.
Location agreement. When both give a structure: 2 same compartment family, 0 different. 1: only one of them has a structure (or neither), so nothing to compare.
Expressed in U2-OS. HPA RNA for the U2OS line: 2 at 10 nTPM or more, 1 at 1 or more, 0 below.

Taggable

Takes an endogenous tag without moving or changing level; a homozygous line is viable.

Tagged elsewhere. 2 if an endogenous tag at this gene already works in another human line, OpenCell (HEK293T, CZ Biohub SF) or the Allen Cell Collection (hiPSC), 1 if not (never 0). What that gives us is evidence the tag is tolerated and a design to copy, not a U2-OS line. Allen lines are distributed through Coriell under an MTA; whether OpenCell shares lines is to be confirmed with CZ Biohub SF. Homozygous viability is not in any database; DepMap essentiality in U2-OS is shown in the detail panel as a hint only.

Quantifiable

Enough copies per cell to count, not so many they saturate; a published number to check against.

Copy number measured. 2 if Beck et al. 2011 measured copies per cell in U2-OS, 1 if not (never 0). The number itself is in the last column; "enough, not too many" is a judgment.

Imageable

The tag signal survives cryo preparation, and the structure is still recognizable.

Not scored yet. No dataset answers this; it gets scored by experiment once there is a shorter list, to help decide between candidates.

Practical

A validated antibody exists; a line can be made in 2027 with tags that exist today.

HPA IF reliability. HPA's own grade for its immunofluorescence: 2 for Enhanced or Supported, 1 for Approved, 0 for Uncertain or no IF data. Proteins with good antibodies elsewhere can score 0 here. Whether a line can be made is not scored.

How locations are mapped to structures

These are judgment calls made while building the table, open to correction. Rule of thumb: yes is a membrane-bound organelle or a discrete body with a recognizable shape in a FIB-SEM volume; partial is recognizable only as a class, needs extra identity, or is a thin filament that is marginal in FIB-SEM; no is diffuse, an aggregate, or not relevant to U2-OS.

How the table is made

The list is every reviewed human protein in UniProt (20,429) plus every gene with HPA subcellular data not already in that set. Each protein appears once per UniProt entry, so a few genes with two entries (TMPO, for example) appear twice.

Locations come from two places: HPA's immunofluorescence annotation and UniProt's subcellular location text. Each HPA location term and each UniProt keyword is mapped to whether it is a structure that can be found in an EM volume (yes, partial, no), and to one of 15 compartment families. The family is what the sidebar filters on.

The total is the plain sum of the six scores, equal weight. Two columns (tagged line, copy number) use 1 for "no" rather than 0, so totals run from 2 to 12. Weighting is deliberately left out for now; it is a working group question.

Script and inputs are in the repository next to this page: score.py, hpa_em_lookup.csv, data/. The table is anchor_scores.tsv.

Known limits

The antibody score only reflects HPA IF reliability. Agreement is judged by family, so "nuclear speckles" and "nuclear lamina" count as agreeing (both nuclear). Copy numbers are from one 2011 mass spectrometry study. OpenCell and Allen lines are in other cell types than U2-OS.
