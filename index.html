The five proposed must-haves, and what the data can say

Anchor proteins are a handful of proteins, 5 to 10, tagged in the U2-OS line so the same molecule can be seen by light microscopy and found in the EM volume of the same cell. The five headings are the proposed must-haves from the Space working group pre-read. Under each, the columns are what public databases can answer today, scored 0, 1 or 2. A low score is something to weigh, not a reason to drop a protein.

Identity

A clear structure in EM, and sources agree on where it sits in U2-OS.

Structure in EM. 2: a clear structure (nuclear envelope, mitochondrial membrane, Golgi, centriole, lysosome…). 1: a broad or mixed class (vesicles, cytoskeleton, chromosome, cell projection). 0: diffuse (cytosol, nucleoplasm) or no location.
Sources agree. 2: HPA and UniProt put it in the same compartment family. 1: only one of them has a location. 0: they disagree.
Expressed in U2-OS. HPA RNA for the U2OS line: 2 at 10 nTPM or more, 1 at 1 or more, 0 below.

Taggable

Takes an endogenous tag without moving or changing level; a homozygous line is viable.

Tagged line exists. 2 if OpenCell (HEK293T) or the Allen Cell Collection (hiPSC) already has an endogenously tagged line, 1 if not. Tag tolerance and viability are not in any database. DepMap essentiality in U2-OS is shown in the detail panel as a hint only.

Quantifiable

Enough copies per cell to count, not so many they saturate; a published number to check against.

Copy number known. 2 if Beck et al. 2011 measured copies per cell in U2-OS, 1 if not. The number itself is in the last column; "enough, not too many" is a judgment.

Imageable

The tag signal survives cryo preparation, and the structure is still recognizable.

Not scored. No dataset answers this; it needs an experiment.

Practical

A validated antibody exists; a line can be made in 2027 with tags that exist today.

HPA antibody. HPA immunofluorescence reliability: 2 for Enhanced or Supported, 1 for Approved, 0 for Uncertain or no IF data. Proteins with good antibodies elsewhere can score 0 here. Whether a line can be made is not scored.

How the table is made

The list is every reviewed human protein in UniProt (20,429) plus every gene with HPA subcellular data not already in that set. Each protein appears once per UniProt entry, so a few genes with two entries (TMPO, for example) appear twice.

Locations come from two places: HPA's immunofluorescence annotation and UniProt's subcellular location text. Each HPA location term and each UniProt keyword is mapped to whether it is a structure that can be found in an EM volume (yes, partial, no), and to one of 15 compartment families. The family is what the sidebar filters on.

The total is the plain sum of the six scores, maximum 12, with equal weight. Weighting is deliberately left out for now; it is a working group question.

Script and inputs are in the repository next to this page: score.py, hpa_em_lookup.csv, data/. The table is anchor_scores.tsv.

Known limits

The antibody score only reflects HPA IF reliability. Agreement is judged by family, so "nuclear speckles" and "nuclear lamina" count as agreeing (both nuclear). Copy numbers are from one 2011 mass spectrometry study. OpenCell and Allen lines are in other cell types than U2-OS.
